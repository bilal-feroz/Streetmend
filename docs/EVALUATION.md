## Limits of this evaluation

* The fixtures are small by design: they test that each claim holds, not how the engine scales. The Charlotte cases measure runtime on a 2,042-node graph.
* The baselines are simple, documented heuristics. They are not tuned to fail, and they are reported where they match the exact search.
* No user study and no field validation. The Charlotte barriers come from municipal records of varying age, and the change-check settings are hypothetical.
